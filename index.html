Label Sticker Studio

200 × 160 mm sheet · 9 columns × 16 rows · 20 × 8 mm labels

1. Sequence

Each sequence can start at a different sticker slot. Overlapping sequences are flagged before PDF export.

The first copy is centered; its duplicate prints in the next sticker slot and is left-justified.

Slots 1-9 remain completely unprinted so the printer can grip the leading edge. Numbering begins at slot 10.

Turn on marking, then tap one position or drag from one corner to another to select a rectangular area. Start on a highlighted position to clear an area. Highlighted positions remain blank in the PDF.

2. Physical sheet layout

Exact reset: 20 mm label + 2 mm gap = 22 mm horizontal pitch; 8 mm label + 2 mm gap = 10 mm vertical pitch.

3. Printer correction

Net horizontal correction = right minus left. Net vertical correction = down minus up.

Use the downloaded PDF: it is exactly one 200 × 160 mm page. Print at 100% / Actual Size with no scaling. Do not force Landscape—the PDF already contains the correct wide orientation.
HP 323dnw warning: its officially supported custom sheets are at least 216 mm long and no more than 148.5 mm wide. A 200 × 160 mm sheet is outside its supported feed range, so physical skew may remain even with a correct PDF.

How to calibrate without wasting labels

  1. Cut plain paper to exactly 200 × 160 mm.
  2. Use Download calibration PDF, open it, and print at Actual Size.
  3. Place it behind the sticker sheet and hold both against a light.
  4. If every box is shifted, change the right/down correction.
  5. If alignment gradually drifts, measure the real pitch and update horizontal or vertical pitch.
